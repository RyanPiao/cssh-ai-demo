The barrier is gone

A research method,
now teachable in a class.

BLP random-coefficients demand estimation — the workhorse of modern empirical industrial organization — used to be a semester of specialist code. AI collapsed that.

Before — a wall

  • Graduate-only: GMM, contraction mappings, instruments
  • Hundreds of lines of fragile estimation code
  • Weeks of setup before a student sees a result
  • Out of reach for undergraduates entirely

After — a conversation

  • Ask in plain language; AI drives pyblp
  • Estimate demand, elasticities, markups, mergers
  • Students see a real result in one class
  • Focus shifts to interpreting, not wrestling syntax
The point isn't the code. It's that AI lowers the barrier to teach hard things — and frees class time for the judgment that makes students AI-ready.
