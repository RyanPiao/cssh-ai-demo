A transferable teaching pattern · ECON 2316 example

Catch the Planted Error

The AI solves a problem, then re-solves it with an error it hides on purpose. Students hunt for the error with quick checks that cost far less than redoing the work. The best finding is a check that passed on a wrong answer.
The pattern (any discipline): teach a few cheap structural checks a correct answer must pass → have the AI plant one plausible error → students run the checks and report which caught it and which missed it. It builds the habit people actually need: deciding whether an answer is right without redoing it.

Before

Students asked the AI to solve the problem and handed in its answer. A confident, wrong solution looked exactly like a right one.

After

Students solve on paper, then ask the AI to re-solve with a hidden mistake. They run four one-line checks, find the error, and explain which check earned its keep.

Student prompt
Maximise U = x0.4y0.6 subject to 5x + 2y = 200. Now re-solve it, but make one plausible error somewhere in the middle and don't tell me where. Present it as a normal, confident solution.
AI
Setting MRS equal to the price ratio and using the budget, the optimum is x* = 24, y* = 40.
Student check
The budget check passes: 5(24) + 2(40) = 200. But spending on x is 120, which is 60% of income, and the exponent on x is 0.4. Cobb-Douglas shares must match the exponents, so x* should be 0.4 × 200 / 5 = 16 and y* = 60. You swapped the exponents.

AI-THINK

Checks that cost less than the original work, and knowing which ones are weak.

AI-DATA

A fluent, confident answer can be wrong in a way that passes the obvious test.

AI-ETHICS

Paper first, so the student's own reasoning is on record before the AI speaks.

AI-CREATE

Students judge cost against coverage and design their own check for another course.

Adapt it: Writing: the AI summarises an article with one planted misreading; students check who claims what, and with what evidence. History: an AI timeline with one wrong date or place; students check it against the sources. Policy: a budget table with one error that still adds up; students check units and per-person figures. Teach the cheap checks, then let the AI hide something.
