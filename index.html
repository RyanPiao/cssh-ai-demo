AI in Teaching

Practical resources to teach with and about AI — keep what makes your teaching yours, and help students become genuinely AI-ready.

AI didn't create a cheating crisis — it exposed how we assess. When a machine can complete a take-home task without learning anything, the task was measuring the product, not the thinking. That's a design problem we know how to solve.

And "AI-ready" isn't "can use ChatGPT." It's judgment: knowing when to use these tools, evaluating whether their output is any good, using them ethically, and adding the human value on top. Not every course must use AI — the goal is that every program gives students some exposure. Ring-fencing AI-free courses is fine.
