AI support across Northeastern

CATLRThe university teaching center: workshops, one-on-one consultations, and AI Instructional Assistants (graduate students who can help build materials; request one through your CATLR liaison). Academic TechnologiesQuestions about which AI tools are approved, and help with the technology in your course. NU Claude PortalUniversity-supported AI access for the Northeastern community, so students don't need a paid account. NULab DITI & AI (CSSH)Our college's Digital Integration Teaching Initiative: slides, handouts, a bibliography, and critical AI literacy materials for humanities and social science courses. Snell Library: Course PlanningAI literacy modules, course-planning resources, and research support you can bring into a class. Institute for Experiential AIUniversity AI experts, courses, and possible guest speakers. Human-Centered AI hubNortheastern's central AI site: strategy, news, and new initiatives such as the AI Makerspace and AI Innovation Lab.
